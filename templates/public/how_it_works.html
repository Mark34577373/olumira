AT YOUR FAMILY’S PACE

From your first step
to your everyday rhythm.

A connected space starts with a few thoughtful choices. Setup is saved, optional steps can be skipped, and you can return later.

{{ steps() }}
01

Start with your space

Create an account where registration is enabled, then confirm your email before using protected care tools. If you received a workspace invitation, sign in with its addressed email and open the invitation again to accept it.

Accounts include a private family workspace. Guided setup lets you use it, select another authorized workspace or explicitly create a separate one.

Use fictional profile and care information during testing. Email delivery depends on the configured operator-run mail service.

02

Make daily support personal

Select or create a care profile using only fictional information. Choose existing routines and boards or adapt a starter. In Care Plans, connect priorities, goals, routines, communication and responsibilities around that profile.

Setup never requires a diagnosis. Optional steps can be skipped; leaving saves completed steps, and unsaved field changes are not retained.

03

Find your everyday rhythm

Your dashboard brings the active workspace and profile into view. Open a routine, use a communication board, record an observation, check responsibilities or prepare a reviewed handoff.

Existing records remain available when you revisit setup. Returning users who completed or intentionally skipped setup can go straight to their dashboard.

04

Invite with intention

Owners manage workspace invitations and membership. Caregivers can contribute within their permissions; viewers have read-only access to shared records and can use supported communication interactions.

Workspace membership determines access. A selected care profile organizes that workspace’s content; it is not a separate permission grant. Share only with people you authorize.
