Clarity before launch.

This is draft product information, not an approved final legal policy. Legal, operational and privacy review is required before a public real-data launch.

Controlled testing uses fictional information only. Olumira is not approved to store real families’ sensitive information.

{% if kind=='privacy' %}

What the application currently stores

Account identity and credential hashes, workspace memberships, optional care profiles, routines, communication content, observations, schedules, responsibilities and shared care history are stored in the configured relational database. Do not enter real sensitive records during testing.

Access and sharing

Authenticated workspace members receive access according to their role. A selected care profile organizes workspace records. Members with access may retain downloaded reports; removing access does not revoke copies already downloaded.

Email and device features

Account and invitation messages use an encrypted email queue and the configured delivery provider. In local development, messages are available only through the private development inbox. Browser speech requests supported local voices; the device and browser’s implementation must be reviewed separately.

Retention and account controls

Authorized account settings include export and closure workflows. Closure anonymizes account identity and revokes access; shared history, authorship references, delivery metadata and backups may remain. Archiving records does not erase them. Do not assume every copy is deleted.

Public contact information

The public contact form is unavailable. It does not deliver or persist submissions. Ordinary infrastructure access metadata may still be logged; application request logging excludes query values and form bodies.

Review still required

Operator identity and contact, lawful basis and notices, applicable rights procedures, retention schedules, hosting and email-provider arrangements, cookies, backup deletion and international transfers must be assessed and finalized before real-data use. No legal compliance or security certification is claimed.

Purpose and testing limits

Olumira provides organizational tools for everyday support. Testing is limited to fictional information. Features may change, and local checks do not establish hosted availability or operational readiness.

Appropriate access

Use only accounts and workspaces you are authorized to access. Do not share credentials, attempt to bypass permissions or place sensitive records in test environments. Owners should invite only authorized collaborators.

Not medical or emergency support

Olumira does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medical advice, clinical validation or emergency monitoring. Descriptive activity summaries are not health recommendations.

Availability and support

No production service-level or response-time commitment is made by this draft. Controlled testers should use the contact channel supplied by their test organizer. Public contact submissions are currently unavailable.

Legal review still required

The legal entity and contact, eligibility and consent, intellectual property and licensing, acceptable use, payment terms if introduced, liability, termination, disputes and governing law require review before final terms can be issued. This page does not substitute for that review.
