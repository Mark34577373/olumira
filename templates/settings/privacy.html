Export your information

Personal JSON includes your account and personal preferences, bookmarks and inbox across your workspaces. It excludes shared care records and other caregivers. Owners can export all care profiles and shared records in the selected workspace. Credentials, email payloads and invitation tokens are always excluded.

Retention and backups

Read and dismissed notifications are removed after your configured retention period when the operator runs maintenance. Unread notifications remain. Expired download access is also removed by maintenance; export files are never stored on the server.

Archived care records and immutable history are retained until an operator reviews erasure. Account deletion anonymizes account details, removes personal preferences and revokes sessions and memberships. Historical authorship identifiers remain in shared records.

Workspace closure revokes all member access and invitations immediately. It does not erase shared records. An audit record and an operator-review request are retained. There is no automatic physical purge or public restore.

Local SQLite backup support exists. Backups may contain pre-deletion information. The application does not provide encryption or scheduled backup retention; deployment operators must configure storage access, retention and restoration. Ask your operator about backups for your deployment.
